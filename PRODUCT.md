# Nightfall

## Register
brand

## Purpose
An ambient, interactive ASCII night sky, built from the user's request for shifting stars and moon elements. Design assumptions: visitors want a quiet moment of visual exploration; no account or backend is needed.

## Direction
Quiet, tactile, celestial. A person unwinding at their laptop after dark watches a gently moving field of characters. The scene is the main content, with unobtrusive, useful controls. Avoid conventional marketing sections and decorative cards.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, descriptive canvas alternative, reduced-motion support, and an explicit pause control.

## Current revision
The user renamed the wordmark to NCS and explicitly selected the sky/hero section, customization panel, and footer for removal. The current page retains the header and about dialog.

## Brand identity
The website is for No Code Society (NCS). Use the user-supplied pixelated white crescent logo at `dist/assets/no-code-society-logo.png`. Nightfall was an initial design concept, not the organization's identity. Do not describe NCS as a sky experiment or invent its history, membership, events, or affiliations.
